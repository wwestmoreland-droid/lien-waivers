Past paid invoices

Invoices that were already paid when a job started being tracked don't get a waiver on their own. Pick the ones that should, by paid date, and click Prepare. Each one gets the waiver the rules give for a paid invoice at that point in the job (unconditional partial, or unconditional final for the last payment), and its request email waits in the Outbox for you to send.
